Installation & Commissioning

Structured Setup Across Residential, Commercial & Plant Sites

Proper mechanical integration protects membrane life and maintains steady hydraulic behavior. AquaPure coordinates installation planning around your site's plumbing layout, drainage access, and feed pressure conditions.

  • ✓Domestic purifier installation: Clean mounting, feed-line connection, and initial system flushing
  • ✓Commercial RO system installation: Compact utility placement for kitchens, offices, and clinics
  • ✓Industrial RO plant installation: Vessel assembly, skid piping, and pre-treatment alignment
  • ✓System setup & commissioning: Flow balance verification and operator handover walkthrough
  • ✓Requirement-based planning: Site constraints reviewed prior to installation scheduling
