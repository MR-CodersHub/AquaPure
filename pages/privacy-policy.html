Privacy Policy

How AquaPure handles enquiry submissions, consultation inputs, and local display preferences with clear respect for your privacy.

Last Updated: 2026 No Personal Data Resale Transparent Local Controls
01

Information We Collect

We collect only the details you choose to share directly when submitting a product consultation, maintenance, or water testing request, along with essential technical information used to display the website properly.

  • Contact details you provide in forms (such as full name, email address, and phone number).
  • Project context including customer segment, equipment category, or water source enquiry notes.
  • Standard server access diagnostics when hosted, plus local browser display settings.
02

How We Use Information

Information submitted to AquaPure is used strictly to evaluate water purification needs, prepare consultation guidance, coordinate installation or preventive care, and respond to your questions.

  • Match your enquiry with suitable residential, commercial, or industrial water treatment solutions.
  • Arrange requested water quality testing discussions, site assessments, or service schedules.
  • Improve website clarity, navigation, and technical documentation.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party brokers.

03

Browser Storage & Front-End Forms

AquaPure saves lightweight display preferences locally in your browser via localStorage (aquapure-theme for Light/Dark mode and aquapure-dir for LTR/RTL layout) so your chosen viewing experience persists across pages.

On this front-end demonstration website, interactive forms validate inputs client-side without external database transmission until connected to a dedicated backend service.

04

Retention & Safeguards

When deployed with active customer support workflows, enquiry records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill consultations, ongoing maintenance support, and applicable legal obligations. Access is limited to authorized personnel handling your request.

05

Your Choices & Privacy Enquiries

You may clear local browser preferences at any time via your browser settings, or contact AquaPure directly to ask questions, update submitted contact details, or request removal of enquiry records via our Contact page.
