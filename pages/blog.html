Educational Highlight

Core Water Treatment Concepts at a Glance

Understanding basic treatment terminology helps homeowners and facility managers ask clearer questions during consultations.

Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) represents the combined concentration of dissolved minerals, salts, and ions present in water. While TDS alone does not identify every contaminant, evaluating TDS alongside source origin helps determine whether simple particulate filtration or reverse osmosis is appropriate.
Suspended sand, silt, and free chlorine can clog or oxidize fine RO membrane layers rapidly. Sediment filters and activated carbon vessels protect downstream membranes so systems operate smoothly between service visits.
Ion-exchange water softeners specifically target hardness minerals (calcium and magnesium) that cause scale in pipes, heaters, and laundry equipment, whereas RO addresses broader dissolved solids for drinking or low-TDS process water.
